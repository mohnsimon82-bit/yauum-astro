### A simple process that keeps your order clear before production starts

Production goes better when both sides agree on the same garment. We confirm the style, materials, sample, labels, packing, and quality points before bulk work begins.

#### Send Your Product Details

Share the product type, quantity, price target, photos, tech pack, size range, fabric idea, labels, packing needs, and delivery market.

#### Choose Materials and Details

Confirm fabric weight, color, handfeel, buttons, zippers, drawcords, labels, printing, embroidery, stitching, lining, and packaging.

#### Make and Review the Sample

Use the sample to check fit, measurements, workmanship, fabric behavior, brand details, and the overall look before bulk production.

#### Approve or Revise

Adjust measurements, trims, fabric, labels, artwork placement, packing, or finishing until the approved sample becomes the production standard.

#### Produce, Check, and Pack

Bulk production follows the approved sample through cutting, sewing, finishing, inspection, folding, packing, and shipment preparation.

### Your products should arrive ready to sell, pack, or ship
